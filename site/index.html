Private by default

Your voice, your choice.

Run it 100% on your Mac, or flip to the cloud for speed. Switch anytime from the menu bar.

Offline🟢 Default

Dictation and AI writing run entirely on-device. No internet, no keys, no cost.

  • Whisper transcription on Apple Silicon
  • Local AI writing (gpt-oss:20b)
  • Works on a plane, nothing uploaded
Online🔵 Optional

Runs on Groq's cloud, fast on any Mac, no downloads. One free key.

  • Whisper-large-v3 dictation
  • Sharper AI writing (gpt-oss-120b)
  • Only what you dictate is ever sent
🔒

Nothing leaves your Mac.

In Offline mode your voice never touches the internet. It's transcribed and written entirely on your device. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, ever.
