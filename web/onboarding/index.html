Online keys

Two free keys.

Online mode streams dictation through AssemblyAI and writes/edits with Groq — both free to start, no card. Paste each below; they’re stored encrypted in your macOS Keychain, never uploaded or committed.

🎙️
AssemblyAI — not setStreaming dictation. Free key from assemblyai.com.
🧠
Groq — not setAI write & edit (gpt-oss-120b). Free key from console.groq.com.
🔒 Keychain — keys are entered in a secure macOS prompt and stored encrypted. Skip Groq and AI writing falls back to the on-device model (sharper with Groq).
