# Hero banner — image-generation prompt

Paste this into ChatGPT (GPT-4o image / DALL·E 3), generate, then save the result
as `assets/hero.png` in this repo. Ask for a **wide 16:6 banner (e.g. 1536×576)**.

---

**Prompt:**

> Create a sleek, modern **hero banner** (wide 16:6 landscape) for a free, local
> Mac voice-dictation app called **Voice To Text**. Premium Apple-keynote
> aesthetic: a deep **indigo-to-blue gradient** background with a soft glow and
> generous negative space.
>
> Centerpiece on the left: a glowing white **audio waveform / equalizer** (rounded
> vertical bars of varying heights, like a voice visualizer) flowing into a
> floating "recording pill," echoing a rounded-square app icon that is a blue
> gradient tile with white waveform bars. Suggest sound turning into clean text —
> e.g. a subtle stream of crisp typed words appearing on a minimalist MacBook to
> the right.
>
> **Make the install story the clear secondary focal point** (lower-right): a small
> chat exchange that obviously shows *"just tell Claude and it installs itself."*
> Render it as two stacked chat bubbles with a sparkle ✦ (Claude) avatar:
>   - a user bubble: **"Install Voice To Text for me"**
>   - a Claude reply bubble: **"On it — downloading & setting up… ✓ Done"**
> Above or beside it, a short bold ribbon/label: **"Install in one line — just ask
> Claude Code or Cowork."** Keep this readable and prominent; it's the key message.
>
> Include **4 small, clean feature chips/badges** (simple pill labels with tiny
> icons) reading: **"100% Local & Private"**, **"Free"**, **"Smart Formatting"**,
> **"Tone-Aware ✦"**. Keep any text minimal, perfectly spelled, crisp, and legible
> (sans-serif). If text rendering is unreliable, prefer clean icons over words.
>
> Style: high-end SaaS landing-page hero, flat-meets-subtle-3D, soft shadows and
> glow, microphone motif, very clean and uncluttered, no photographic clutter, no
> human faces. Color palette: indigo, royal blue, white, hints of cyan.

---

**Tip:** image models often garble small text. If the feature chips come out
misspelled, either (a) regenerate, or (b) ask for the banner *without* text and
add the labels yourself in Preview/Figma/Canva. A clean text-light version often
looks more premium anyway.
